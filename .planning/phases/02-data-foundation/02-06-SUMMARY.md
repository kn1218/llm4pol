## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 – Bug] `tacticity_multi_label_canonical` reproduced 0, not ADR-0006's 2**

- **Found during:** Task 2b (STEP 5/6, first validator run after the flip)
- **Issue:** The plan's STEP 2 sketch defined the count as *"the distinct `canonical_psmiles` that both carry at least one originally-empty row and have two or more distinct non-empty labels"*. Implemented literally, that publishes **0** on the pinned snapshot, while ADR-0006's Context table — the stated authority for every number in this plan — states **2**.
- **Measurement that decided it:** on the in-scope rows, 2 canonicals carry two or more distinct non-empty labels; **0** of those also carry an originally-empty row; and 55 canonicals carry an empty row and no label at all (those 55 produce the 60 unresolved rows). So the ADR's "2" is the count of repeat units the rule's guard refuses to resolve *from*, not the count of empty rows the guard left unknown — and the extra conjunct in the plan's sketch was the defect.
- **Fix:** `filters.tacticity_resolution_counts` now counts `multi_label_canonical` over every in-scope canonical with ≥2 distinct non-empty labels. The docstring records why, including the measurement, so the next reader does not re-add the conjunct.
- **Files modified:** `src/llm4pol/data/filters.py`, `tests/test_data_invariants.py` (the synthetic assertion moves 0 → 1: the fixture's `*CC(*)C` occurs isotactic and atactic)
- **Verification:** `finding tacticity_multi_label_canonical: reproduce expected=2 observed=2 reproduced`; `tests/test_data_real_file.py::test_real_tacticity_resolution_counts_match_adr_0006` PASSED
- **Committed in:** `5e7b296` (Task 2b commit)
- **Note for the owner:** this is a wording slip in ADR-0006's Consequences, not in its Context table. The Consequences say the 2 multi-label SMILES *"are therefore left `unknown` by the rule's own guard"*; on this snapshot none of the 2 carries an empty row, so nothing of theirs was left unknown. The Context table's number (2) reproduces; the causal clause beside it does not describe this snapshot. No action was taken on the ADR text.

**2. [Rule 3 – Blocking] The republished notes exceeded the report's 80-character cell cap**
