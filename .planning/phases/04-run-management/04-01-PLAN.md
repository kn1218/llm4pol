the entry point with what it drives (plan 04-02, whose single task is the tracer). This plan is
the half of the tracer that has no entry point of its own; it is not a layer built for later use,
and plan 04-02 is the first plan after which a user can run something.

`create_run_dir`. Task 2 is the ledger with its schema: a ledger without its schema would be a
payload without a guard.
