<objective>
Make the ledger the only state. `resume` reads the record, refuses what it cannot trust, rebuilds
the cache and the budget meter from the recorded `evaluation` events and continues at the first
event that is not yet recorded - inside the open iteration, never by restarting it (Pitfall 5).
A budget refusal becomes a recorded end of the run. `ledger.read` refuses every defect of a line
or of the sequence.

Purpose: charter section 13 M3 exit criterion (1) - a run interrupted and resumed records zero
duplicate events - and the first ADR-0008 guard: a resumed ledger and its `results.csv` equal the
bytes of the uninterrupted run at every event boundary. The research spike measured why: with
state rebuilt from the ledger 13 of 13 crash points were identical, with a fresh cache and meter
7 of 13 (F-61, F-62). With one evaluation event per candidate the reference campaign has 17
events, so this plan tests 18 boundaries.

Output: `resume` as a library function and a verb, `rebuild_state`, the integrity checks of
`ledger.read`, the reference and budget campaigns in `tests/run_support.py`, the A-6 row extended.

Reading of RUN-03 and charter section 7 ("resume continues from the last closed iteration"): the
ledger is append-only, so the events of an interrupted iteration stay; `resume` continues in the
iteration that follows the last closed one and skips what that iteration already recorded. It
never records an `(iteration, beam)` event twice.
