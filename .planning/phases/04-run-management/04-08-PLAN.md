full semantics of `replay`, the one committed fixture ledger whose reduced bytes are pinned on
both CI platforms, and the scanner check over every file a run writes.

Purpose: charter section 13 M3 exit criterion (2) - `replay` byte-identical - and the second
ADR-0008 guard. Cross-platform byte identity is the one fact the research could not verify on
this machine (F-33); the pinned fixture is the test that settles it on the ubuntu-latest leg.

Output: `summary_of`, `render_outputs` and `result_sha256` in `reduce.py`, `replay` in full,
`protocol/schemas/run-summary.json` with its example in the inventory, `tests/test_run_replay.py`,
the committed fixture ledger.

**Twelve files, one task, and why.** The summary, its schema, its example and its inventory entry
are one change (D-39: the writer validates before it writes, and an example without its entry is
unguarded). `replay` is the reader of that file and cannot be tested before it exists. The
fixture pins what `replay` reproduces. Three existing test files change one expectation each
because the run directory gains its fifth file.

`completed`, `completed_iterations` 2,

**Owner notes (choices made within the decisions, stated for review).**
1. `result_sha256` (CONTEXT D-03) is the sha256 of the bytes of `results.csv`, then `usage.json`,
   then `run_summary.json`. The first revision of this plan left `run_summary.json` out; nothing
   prevents including it - the summary holds no hash of itself - and including it makes one
   printed hash cover every reduced file and, through `ledger_sha256` inside the summary, the
   ledger as well. It is printed by `replay` and not stored, because ADR-0008 item 6 lists what
   `run_summary.json` carries. Anyone holding the three tracked files can recompute it.
2. `status` takes the values `open`, `completed`, `budget_exhausted`.
3. The fixture ids are hashes of public repeat units and tacticities, so some of them are also
   ids of the pinned table. The guard of this plan is therefore "fixture ids and invented values
   only", not "no id of the pinned table".
