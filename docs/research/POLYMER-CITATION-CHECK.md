## Important 2025-2026 items the landscape missed

Simulation oracles / automated MD:
- ADEPT-PolyGraphMT (Alosious, ... Luo 2026, arXiv:2603.27106; code github.com/sobinalosious/ADEPT) - SMILES -> PySIMM/GAFF2 -> LAMMPS + Psi4 HPC array workflow computing density, Tg (ML-seeded), Cp, TC, dielectric, moduli; directly competes with RadonPy as a Tg oracle.
- PolyRapid (Carbone group 2026, arXiv:2603.05362; github.com/paolacarbone/PolyRapid) - GROMACS/OPLS-AA automated melt equilibration + Tg for 103 homopolymers with experimental comparison CSV.
- polymerist v1.x (Shirts group, PyPI 2025-2026; Bernat et al. 2026, ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15002977) - OpenFF-based homo/copolymer builder and reproducible OpenMM simulation spec; copolymer-capable, unlike PSP.
- XPB (Chen, Zhang, Xu, JCTC 2025, 21, 347-357; 10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01265) - extendable polymer builder for linear/dendritic/cross-linked structures.
- CGMas (2026, arXiv:2608.06694) - multi-agent framework automating coarse-grained polymer MD.
- Bhati, Afzal, Chew, Browning, Halls, Polymers 2026, 18(14), 1727 (10.3390/polym18141727; data Zenodo 19242815) - binary-copolymer Tg ML (RMSE ~14 K, R2 ~0.98 on 666 homo+copolymer points), 148,365 enumerated copolymers, MD validation R2 0.89 - the closest existing analogue of the LLM4POL copolymer Tg task.

Polymer agents / LLMs:
- PolyFusionAgent (2026, arXiv:2605.26543) - multimodal foundation model + autonomous assistant for property prediction and inverse design.
- PolyTrace (Wu & Zhou 2026, ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15004805) - grounded LLM reasoning for data-efficient, faithful Tg prediction.
- PolyBench: "Teaching and Evaluating LLMs to Reason About Polymer Design Related Tasks" (2026, arXiv:2601.16312) - benchmark and fine-tuned Qwen/Phi models for polymer design reasoning.
- "Towards Discovery of Polymers for Insulin Delivery via Physics-Grounded Agentic Workflows" (Otun et al. 2026, arXiv:2605.18831).
- "Can LLMs Predict Polymer Physics Just by Reading Synthesis and Processing Prose?" (2026, arXiv:2605.08255).
- HiPoly (2026, arXiv:2609.02746) - hierarchical polymer-native graph framework (G2RINS) covering stochastic connectivity, composition and molecular weight, with generative design and MD validation; includes a PFAS-free design case.
- HHT (Huang & Liu 2026, ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15000246) - natural-language-augmented polymer property prediction.

Interpretability / design rules:
- "Orchestrating explainable AI, ChatGPT, and human expertise: a framework for extracting polymer design guidelines" (J. Mater. Chem. A 2026, 10.1039/D5TA06120B) - SHAP + LLM descriptor interpretation -> actionable AEM design rules; closest analogue of LLM4MOF-style interpretable rule extraction.
- Multi-Granular Rationale-Guided Molecular LLM (2026, arXiv:2608.10480) - substructure-level rationales for property prediction.

Benchmarks / data infrastructure:
- PolyMetriX (npj Comput. Mater. 2025, s41524-025-01823-y; Zenodo 14980914) - curated 7367-point Tg benchmark with LOCO and Tg-extrapolation splitters; documents that cross-dataset Tg MAE ranges 13.8-214.8 K.
- OpenPoly (Wang et al., Chin. J. Polym. Sci. 2025, 10.1007/s10118-025-3402-y) - 3985 curated experimental polymer-property points, 26 properties.
- PolyMon (2026, arXiv:2603.13303; github.com/fate1997/polymon) - unified benchmarking framework (Tg 7,367 pts, FFV, TC, density, Rg) with delta-learning and multi-fidelity.
- CoPolyGNN (Pinheiro et al., ECML-PKDD 2026, 10.1007/978-3-032-06118-8_25; github.com/CIDAG/CoPolyGNN) - copolymer GNN with multi-task auxiliary learning over experimental and simulated Tg sets.
