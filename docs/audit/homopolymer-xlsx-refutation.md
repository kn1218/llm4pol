## What the audit missed (extra findings, all computed)

X1. **Computed, not typed, property values.** 155 conductivity values are bitwise exactly `1/rho` and 137 resistivities bitwise `1/sigma`; float-noise patterns (`...99999`, `...00000x`) appear in 186 conductivity, 70 resistivity, 56 density (e.g. 1.19499999999999), 21 modulus (3.09999999999999), 28 stress and 6 elongation values. Tg/Tm have 0 such values. So the electrical/mechanical columns passed through arithmetic (unit conversion or reciprocal) before export.
X2. **Famous-polymer data exists under other pids** (see C8 caveat): P322231 PS Tg 367 K; P374137 PDMS Tg 151 K, density 0.97; P350006 PTFE elongation 300 / stress 0.021 GPa; P350007 PVDF 2 props.
X3. **Repeat-unit multiplicity escapes CanonSMILES dedup**: dimerising every 2-star unit (13,506 dimerisable) and looking it up finds 2 pairs that are the same polymer at different unit lengths: P312047 "cyclo-polyethene" `*C*` vs P010001 `*CC*`; P050006 PTFE `*C(*)(F)F` vs P350006 `*C(F)(F)C(*)(F)F`. 12 rows have <=2 heavy atoms; 2 have a single heavy atom (P312047 `*C*`, P080177 polysulfur `*S*`).
X4. **2 fully duplicated rows** (every column except pid identical): P330074/P330075 and P472170/P472173 (Tg 296 both).
X5. **Stereo lost in canonicalisation for 5 rows**: polymer_smiles carries @ / \ marks but CanonSMILES does not: P342268, P342368, P372081, P372966, P372967 (35 vs 30 stereo-bearing rows).
X6. **Duplicate names carry conflicting Tg**: 78 duplicate-iupac groups have >=2 Tg values, 77 of them conflicting, max spread 212 K.
X7. **Structure bridge to the CSV is much larger than the audit's 139 rows**: 1,776 xlsx rows (1,725 unique RDKit-canonical structures) equal one of the 4,782 canonical CSV monomer SMILES (smiles1/smiles2); 1,059 of those rows have Tg, 279 have zero properties. This is a usable homopolymer <-> copolymer-component link.
X8. **CSV component-id irregularities** (relevant to C2): component1+component2 non-null = 48,696 (audit text says 48,299); 45,636 start with `CU` but 37 are malformed: `CU0700076` (7 digits) x15, `CU30010` (5 digits) x7, `CU900684(CU040048/CU040049)` x5, `CU900688(CU040048/CU040055)` x3, `CU60002`, `CU(L1a)`, `CU(L1b)`; and 6 component values are P90 polymer ids (`P904006` x4, `P900017(CU010001/CU030001)`, `P905130[B(CU060017/CU010072)/CU020001]`).
X9. **Order-of-magnitude entries**: 109/1,268 resistivity and 101/1,296 conductivity values are exact powers of ten. Tg_K has 914 distinct values; mode 503 K (53 rows), then 333 K (49), 323 K (45).
X10. **Tm-without-Tg by class**: P09 315, P10 289, P37 222, P07 210, P39 190 (of 2,222).
X11. **Mechanical self-inconsistency beyond stress>modulus**: stress/modulus ratio > 0.2 in 35 rows, > 0.5 in 17 (median 0.040); 20 modulus values < 0.01 GPa; 9 stress values < 0.001 GPa; 8 rows combine elongation > 500 % with modulus > 1 GPa (e.g. P150022 dimethylbisphenol-A polycarbonate 715 % / 2.3 GPa).
X12. 34 of the 192 duplicate-structure rows have zero properties (they add nothing but noise to a dedup step).
X13. The file is pid-sorted except 6 descents (audit's non-claim statement confirmed).
